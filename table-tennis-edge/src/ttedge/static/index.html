TT Edge table tennis line scanner

Mispriced lines

Prices that beat a fair value built from the other books' margin-free prices, the point model and (when players have enough history) the ratings. Click a row for the full match board. Edge = expected profit per unit staked; Stake = capped fractional Kelly, as a share of bankroll.

Cross-book arbitrage

Best prices across books whose implied probabilities sum to under 100%. Stake the shares shown to lock in the return whatever happens. Arbs between soft books are often stale lines that get voided or cut; check limits and void rules (retirements, walkovers) before staking.

Matches on the board

Fair match price from the blended point probability. Click a match to see every book's price for every market against fair.

Match pricer

Enter a match-winner price pair (decimal, +American or fractional). The margin is removed, the fair price is converted to a point-win probability, and every derivative market is priced from it. Or enter the point probability directly.

Player ratings

Point-based ratings learned from every point in the results file. P(point) v avg is the chance of winning a point against an average player; small differences matter: 52% per point is about 64% per best-of-5 match.

Walk-forward backtest

Replays past matches in time order: ratings only know earlier results, bets use the opening prices, and closing prices measure closing-line value (CLV). CLV is the faster and more reliable signal than ROI.

Run the backtest to see results (takes a few seconds per thousand matches).
